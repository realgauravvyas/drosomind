About DROSOMIND & Google Research Connectomics

Key Citation:
"Sexual dimorphism in the complete connectome of the Drosophila male central nervous system"
Cell, September 2026. Google Research, HHMI Janelia, & Collaborators.

The Connectomics Milestone

In September 2026, Google Research and the Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) Janelia Research Campus released the complete synaptic wiring diagram of the male fruit fly’s central nervous system (CNS), creating the largest high-resolution brain map in history.

Breakthrough Scale

  • 166,000+ Reconstructed Neurons: Automated 3D segmentation via Flood-Filling Networks (FFNs) and the PATHFINDER AI system.
  • 125+ Million Synaptic Connections: Synapses annotated and human-verified across the Central Brain, Optic Lobes, and Ventral Nerve Cord.
  • First Full Brain-to-Body Spinal Link: Maps descending commands from the brain through the Ventral Nerve Cord (VNC) directly into flight motor pools, legs, and courtship singing muscles.

What DROSOMIND Achieves

Rather than simply visualizing a static 3D point cloud or building another detached puzzle game, DROSOMIND creates a closed-loop in-silico cybernetic life simulator:

  • Biophysical Spiking SNN: Leaky integrate-and-fire membrane dynamics with authentic neurotransmitters (ACh, GABA, Glutamate, Dopamine).
  • Bio-Acoustic Courtship Synthesizer: Translates motor neuron spikes into the male fly's authentic 34Hz pulse repetition song and 160Hz sinusoidal flight hum.
  • Ring Attractor Mental Compass: Simulates the 16 wedges of the Ellipsoid Body (E-PG neurons) that maintain spatial heading.
  • Sexual Dimorphism Explorer: Dissects the male-specific P1 courtship hub and AOTU008 axonal arborizations.
  • Interactive Ablation Sandbox: Silence individual neurons with a virtual laser micro-beam to observe behavioral circuit breakdown in real-time.
